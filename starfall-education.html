Partnership Proposal

Stimul8 and Starfall

Early reading, rewarded at home

Why this partnership

Starfall has long helped children learn to read. Stimul8 could let a parent turn a finished Starfall lesson into a reward their child earns, a natural next step that keeps children coming back.

What Starfall could gainWhat families get
More children coming back to readA gentle goal for early readers
A reason for parents to stay involvedA reward they choose
Nothing to change in the productReading progress to celebrate
How it would work

Simple for parents, nothing changes in Starfall

1
A child finishes a Starfall lesson.
2
The parent confirms it in the Stimul8 app. Later, this confirmation could come straight from Starfall through a connection we would build together.
3
The child earns the reward the parent chose and can see their progress in the Stimul8 app.
What we would build together

A shared way to confirm progress

Agree together which Starfall milestones a parent can turn into a reward, starting with the parent confirming them by hand.

A connection built together

If it works for families, design a simple link so Starfall progress can reach Stimul8 automatically, with the parent still in control.

A small pilot

Test it with a small group of Starfall families and measure together whether it helps children keep going.

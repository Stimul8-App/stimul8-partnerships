Stimul8 x Kahoot!

Real rewards fuel engagement on the world's leading learning platform

Why This Partnership

Kahoot!'s 9 billion annual participants represent an unmatched audience of engaged learners hungry for motivation beyond points and badges. Integrating Stimul8's parent-controlled reward system transforms Kahoot! completions into tangible incentives, deepening engagement while giving parents a tool to reinforce learning at home and in classrooms. Together, we amplify both platforms' core missions: making learning irresistible and giving families control over motivation.

How It Works

Simple to connect. Powerful results.

1

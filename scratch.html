Scratch's 100+ million learners are primed for structured motivation—they already love project-based learning. How It Works

Simple to connect. Powerful results.

1
A child creates, shares, or completes a Scratch project—remixing, coding, debugging, and publishing their work to the platform.
2
The parent confirms the project completion in Stimul8, or the two platforms exchange completion data through a direct integration built together.
3
Stimul8 triggers the reward the parent set—badge, screen time, pocket money—and the child earns it instantly.

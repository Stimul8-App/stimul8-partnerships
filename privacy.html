How we handle your details

Stimul8 EdTech Limited · Last updated 3 September 2026

If you've received an email from us about a possible partnership, this page explains what information we hold about you, where we got it, why we're allowed to use it, and how to make us stop. It's written to be read, not to be survived.

Who we are

Stimul8 EdTech Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16114532), registered office 7 Bell Yard, London WC2A 2JR, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the information described here.

Questions, or anything below that you'd like to exercise: jendi@stimul8.app.

What we hold about you

  • Your name
  • Your job title and the company you work for
  • Your work email address
  • Your public professional profile URL (usually LinkedIn)
  • A record of any emails we've sent you and any reply you've sent us

That's the complete list. We hold no personal contact details, no home address, no financial information, and nothing about you outside your professional role.

Where we got it

We did not get this information from you, which is why we're telling you about it now.

It came from one or more of: Explorium, a business-to-business contact data provider; Apollo, Hunter or ZeroBounce, business email verification services; or publicly available sources such as your company's website or your public professional profile.

Why we're using it, and on what basis

We're contacting you because your role at your company suggests you'd be the right person to consider a possible partnership between your organisation and ours. That's the only reason we hold your details.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (UK GDPR and EU GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) — specifically our interest in approaching relevant businesses about a commercial partnership. We've weighed that against your interests and privacy: the data is limited to your professional role, the contact is relevant to your job, the volume is small and individually reviewed, and you can stop it instantly. You can ask us for a copy of that assessment.

We do not sell your data, share it for anyone else's marketing, or use it to build a profile of you.

How to make us stop — and we mean stop.

Reply to any email from us with “no thanks”, or email jendi@stimul8.app. No form, no account, no explanation needed.

We'll record it the same day and never contact you again about this. This is an absolute right where you're in the UK or EU (GDPR Article 21(2)) — there's no balancing test and no expiry, and we apply the same rule to everyone regardless of where they are.

If you ask us not to contact you again, we keep the minimum needed to honour that — usually just your email address on a suppression list. We keep it for that reason only: so that we can recognise you and not contact you again by mistake. We do not use it for anything else. Everything else we hold about you is deleted.

Your other rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of what we hold, correct anything wrong, delete it, restrict what we do with it, or provide it in a portable format. You can also object to our use of it at any time — see above.

Email jendi@stimul8.app and we'll respond within one month. There's no charge.

If you think we've handled your data badly, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or to the data protection authority in your own country. We'd rather you told us first so we can fix it.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after our last meaningful contact with you, then we delete it. If you tell us to stop, we delete everything except the minimal record needed to keep you suppressed.

Where it's stored

In business tools operated by providers in the United States and Europe (our contact database, our email provider, and our workflow automation service), under the safeguards required for international transfers. Access is limited to the people at Stimul8 who need it.

Changes

If we change how any of this works, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

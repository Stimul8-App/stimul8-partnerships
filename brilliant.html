Stimul8 x Brilliant

Why This Partnership

Brilliant's interactive STEM learning platform perfectly complements Stimul8's motivation system, creating a powerful combination of rigorous education and gamified rewards that drives deeper engagement. This partnership enables Brilliant to significantly boost user retention and completion rates while providing Stimul8 access to a highly motivated community of learners who thrive on challenging academic content and structured problem-solving.

How It Works

Children engage with Brilliant's interactive STEM lessons, complete problem-solving challenges, and progress through course modules on Brilliant's platform
Stimul8 tracks key learning milestones including lesson completions, problem-solving accuracy, learning streaks, and course progress through Brilliant's API integration
Students earn Stimul8 rewards including points, achievement badges, and unlockable content based on their consistent learning engagement and academic progress
